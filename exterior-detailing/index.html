What We Do

A hand wash, done properly.

Automatic washes drag grit across your paint with brushes and leave the bugs, tar and road film behind. We start with a foam pre-wash to loosen dirt before anything touches the paint, then wash and dry by hand.

Maintenance Wash & Dry

Foam pre-wash, hand wash, wheels and tires cleaned, hand dry, exterior windows and tire dressing. The right choice for regular upkeep.

Full Exterior Detail

Everything in the Maintenance Wash, plus exterior trim dressed and protected, bug and tar removal, door jambs cleaned, and a spray sealant that protects the paint for 3 to 6 months.

Paint decontamination add-ons

Even after a good wash, paint can feel rough to the touch. That's contamination bonded to the surface. Two add-ons take care of it:

  • Iron / fallout decontamination dissolves embedded iron particles from brake dust and rail dust. From $40.
  • Clay treatment lifts bonded contaminants so the paint is smooth again. From $60.
  • Paint Decontamination Package bundles both. From $90.

Questions

Exterior detailing FAQs.

How long does the spray sealant last?

The spray sealant in the Full Exterior Detail protects the paint for about 3 to 6 months, depending on weather and how the vehicle is stored and washed.

What's the difference between a car wash and an exterior detail?

A car wash cleans the surface. An exterior detail goes further: foam pre-wash and hand wash to protect the paint, bug and tar removal, door jambs, trim protection and a sealant that keeps the finish protected for months.

Do I need paint decontamination?

If your paint feels rough after a wash, or you see tiny orange specks on lighter colors, contamination is bonded to it. The iron and clay add-ons remove it and leave the paint smooth.

Do you clean wheels and tires?

Yes. Both exterior tiers include wheels and tires cleaned and tire dressing.
