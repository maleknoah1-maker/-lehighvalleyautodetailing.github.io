How It Works

Set it and forget it.

  1. Start with a Full Detail. Any Full Detail at the normal price gets your car fully clean. Plan billing starts after that visit.
  2. Pick your schedule. Monthly or every 2 weeks, drop-off in Coopersburg or mobile in Zone 1.
  3. We keep it that way. We text you to confirm each visit. Your card is billed automatically at the start of each plan month, on your signup date. No contract, cancel anytime.

Questions

Plan questions.

How does the Maintenance Plan work?

Your first visit is any Full Detail at the normal price, so the car starts fully clean. After that, you pick a Monthly or Every 2 Weeks plan and we come back on schedule for a maintenance visit: a basic interior clean plus a hand wash. You're billed automatically by card at the start of each plan month, on the date you signed up, and can cancel anytime.

How much is the Maintenance Plan?

Monthly is $99, $119 or $139 a month for a car or sedan, SUV or truck, or large SUV or van. Every 2 Weeks is $189, $229 or $269 a month. That saves $16 to $26 a month on the monthly plan and $41 to $61 a month on the every 2 weeks plan compared with booking each visit separately.

Why do I need a Full Detail first?

Maintenance visits are priced for a car that's kept clean. The Full Detail resets everything first, so every plan visit after that is quick, consistent and keeps the car looking new.

Can plan visits be mobile?

Yes, in Zone 1 (within about 15 miles of Coopersburg) for $20 per visit, half the normal travel fee. Plan customers outside Zone 1 drop off in Coopersburg.

What happens in the winter?

Your plan keeps going all winter. On a day that's under 40°F or raining, we still do the interior on your scheduled day and make up the exterior wash on the next day that's 40°F or warmer that month. If there isn't one, you get a free exterior wash added to the next month.

When am I charged?

Plans are billed in advance at the start of each plan month, on the date you signed up. For example, if you sign up on the 12th, you're charged on the 12th of each month. You get an emailed receipt every time.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month to month with no contract. Cancel anytime before your next billing date by calling, texting or emailing us, and you won't be charged again.

Are add-ons included?

Add-ons like pet hair removal, leather conditioning or headliner cleaning aren't included, but you can add them to any plan visit at the normal add-on price.
