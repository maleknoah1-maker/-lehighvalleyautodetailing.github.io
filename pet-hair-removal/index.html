Why It's Separate

Why a vacuum isn't enough.

Every interior package includes a standard vacuum, which picks up loose hair on the surface. Hair that's been pressed into carpet, cloth seats and cargo liners is different: it takes specialized tools and extra time to pull out.

How to add it

  • Choose any interior package or full detail.
  • Mention pet hair in the notes when you request a time, or tell us before we begin.
  • Not sure how much there is? Text a photo to (484) 719-1144 and we'll quote it.

Where pet hair hides

Cargo areas and back seats where dogs ride, carpet edges and seat seams, under and between the seats, and cloth headrests. Seat removal for full floor access ($40) is available when hair has worked its way under the seats.

Questions

Pet hair FAQs.

Isn't pet hair removal part of a normal interior detail?

Every interior package includes a standard vacuum, which removes loose hair. Embedded hair woven into the carpet and upholstery takes specialized tools and extra time, so it's a separate add-on.

How much does pet hair removal cost?

$30 to $50, depending on how much hair there is. Text a photo to (484) 719-1144 for a quote before you book.

Can I book pet hair removal by itself?

It's an add-on, so it's paired with an interior package or full detail. That way the rest of the interior gets cleaned at the same time.

Will I be charged a surcharge for pet hair?

No. Pet hair is covered by this add-on rather than the heavily-soiled surcharge.
