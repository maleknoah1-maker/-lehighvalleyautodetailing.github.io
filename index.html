About Us

Lehigh Valley Car Detailing,
Done Right.

Lehigh Valley Auto Detailing is a home-based detailing service in Coopersburg, PA. You drop your vehicle off with us, and it gets our full attention, with no rushing and no shortcuts.

We specialize in complete vehicle detailing, inside and out, using professional grade equipment to reach every crevice, fiber, and surface. Whether it's a daily driver or a weekend vehicle you want kept pristine, we treat every job with the same level of care.

Serving Coopersburg, Allentown, Bethlehem, Center Valley, Emmaus, Quakertown, and the greater Lehigh Valley. Locally owned and operated.
