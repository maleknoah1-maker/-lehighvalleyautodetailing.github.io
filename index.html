About Us

Detailing Done
The Right Way.

Lehigh Valley Auto Detailing is a stationary interior detailing service based in Coopersburg, PA. Customers bring their vehicles to us — no mobile chaos, no compromises. Every detail is performed in a controlled environment with the full attention your vehicle deserves.

We specialize exclusively in interior detailing, using professional-grade equipment to reach every crevice, fiber, and surface. Whether it's a daily driver or a weekend vehicle you want kept pristine, we treat every job with the same level of care.

Serving Coopersburg, Allentown, Bethlehem, Center Valley, Emmaus, and the greater Lehigh Valley. Locally owned and operated.
