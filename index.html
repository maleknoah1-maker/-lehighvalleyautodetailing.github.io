About Us

Detailing Done
The Right Way.

Lehigh Valley Auto Detailing is a stationary detailing service based in Coopersburg, PA. Customers bring their vehicles to us. No mobile chaos, no compromises. Every vehicle gets our full attention, with no rushing and no shortcuts.

We specialize in complete vehicle detailing, inside and out, using professional grade equipment to reach every crevice, fiber, and surface. Whether it's a daily driver or a weekend vehicle you want kept pristine, we treat every job with the same level of care.

Serving Coopersburg, Allentown, Bethlehem, Center Valley, Emmaus, and the greater Lehigh Valley. Locally owned and operated.
