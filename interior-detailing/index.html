What We Do

More than a vacuum and wipe.

Most of what makes a car feel dirty lives where a quick clean never reaches: ground-in dirt in the carpet, grime packed into vents and seams, and film on the dash and glass. Each interior tier builds on the one below it, so you can pick the level your car actually needs.

Basic Interior Clean

A full vacuum of the seats, carpets and trunk, every hard surface wiped and cleaned, and the inside of the windows. Good for a car that's lived-in but not neglected.

Standard Interior Detail Most popular

Everything in Basic, plus steam cleaning for vents, crevices and tight areas, carpet and cloth seat shampoo with extraction, leather wiped and cleaned, door jambs cleaned, and UV protectant on the dash and trim.

Deep Interior Restoration

Everything in Standard, plus multiple extraction passes on all fabric, full steam sanitation of every surface, seatbelts steamed and cleaned, the whole trunk and cargo area including the spare tire well, and every compartment detailed.

Good to know

  • Prices are set by vehicle size: car or sedan, SUV or truck, and large SUV or van.
  • Embedded pet hair is a separate add-on. Every package includes a standard vacuum for loose hair.
  • We never open the glovebox, out of respect for your personal documents.
  • Interior add-ons include leather conditioning ($20), headliner cleaning ($35), seat removal for full floor access ($40) and child seat cleaning ($10 per seat).

Questions

Interior detailing FAQs.

Which interior package do I need?

If the car mostly needs a reset, Basic covers a full vacuum and wipe-down. If the carpets or cloth seats are stained or smell, Standard adds shampoo, extraction and steam cleaning. Deep Restoration is for cars that need everything: multiple extraction passes, full steam sanitation, seatbelts and every compartment. Not sure? Text us a photo at (484) 719-1144 and we'll tell you.

Can you get out old stains?

Many stains come out with shampoo and extraction, and the Deep Restoration tier adds extra passes for stubborn spots. Some stains are permanent, though. If we expect one won't come out completely, we'll tell you up front.

Is pet hair included?

Every package includes a standard vacuum, which picks up loose hair. Hair woven into the carpet and upholstery takes specialized tools and extra time, so it's handled by the Pet Hair Removal add-on ($30 to $50).

Will I be charged extra if my car is really dirty?

Vehicles with excessive dirt, mud or stains may need a $25 to $75 surcharge. It's always discussed with you before we begin, never after.

Do you clean leather seats?

Yes. Standard and Deep Restoration include wiping and cleaning all leather surfaces, and you can add leather conditioning for $20.
