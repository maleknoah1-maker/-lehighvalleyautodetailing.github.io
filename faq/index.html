Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Do I need an appointment, or can I just show up?

By appointment only. This lets us give every vehicle full attention with no rushing. Request a time through our booking form and we'll confirm with you directly.

Do you come to me, or do I bring my car to you?

You bring your car to us in Coopersburg, PA. We're a stationary shop, not a mobile service. Working from a fixed setup means full water pressure and full access, without the limitations mobile vans run into.

How much does a detail cost?

Pricing depends on your vehicle size and the level of service you choose. Full pricing for Interior, Exterior, and Combo packages is listed on our Services page. If you're not sure which tier fits your car, reach out and we'll help you figure it out.

Will I be charged extra if my vehicle is heavily soiled?

In some cases, yes. Vehicles with excessive dirt, pet hair, or stains may incur an additional surcharge of $25 to $75 depending on the work required. This is always communicated to you at drop-off before we begin, never after.

Do you access the glovebox or trunk?

No. We do not open or clean inside the glovebox, out of respect for your personal documents and privacy. If you'd like the trunk cleaned, let us know at drop-off and we'll go over what that includes.

What's the difference between your Interior package tiers?

Basic covers a full vacuum and wipe-down. Standard adds carpet and seat shampoo with extraction, steam cleaning of crevices, and UV protectant. Deep goes further with full steam sanitation, seatbelt cleaning, and detailing every compartment. Full breakdown of what's included in each tier is on our Services page.

What's the difference between a quick car wash and a full detail?

A car wash cleans the surface. A detail goes deeper. We shampoo and extract carpets and seats, steam clean crevices and vents, condition leather, and treat and protect every surface so it lasts. A wash makes your car look clean for a day. A detail actually removes what's built up over months and protects against the next round of wear.
