Do I need an appointment, or can I just show up?

By appointment only. This lets us give every vehicle full attention with no rushing. Request a time through our booking form and we'll confirm with you directly.

Do you come to me, or do I bring my car to you?

Both. You can bring your car to us in Coopersburg, PA (we send the exact drop-off address when we confirm), or book mobile and we'll come to your driveway. Mobile service adds a travel fee of $40 in most of the Lehigh Valley. See full mobile pricing.

How much is the mobile travel fee?

Mobile service is the package price plus a travel fee. Zone 1 is +$40: Coopersburg, Center Valley, Quakertown, Emmaus, Allentown and Bethlehem (about 15 miles from Coopersburg). Zone 2 is +$60: 15 to 25 miles from Coopersburg, for example Easton, Nazareth and Northampton. A second vehicle at the same address has no extra travel fee. Mobile service is available within 25 miles of Coopersburg. Outside that area, drop-off is still available. See mobile pricing.

What do I need to have ready for a mobile detail?

A driveway or parking spot where you're allowed to have work done (some apartment complexes and HOAs don't allow it, so please check first), an outdoor electrical outlet within 50 feet of the vehicle, and a working outdoor water spigot for exterior services.

What happens if it rains on my mobile appointment?

Exterior mobile services depend on the weather. If it's raining or below 40°F, we'll reschedule at no charge, or you can switch to drop-off.

Can you detail my car at my apartment or work?

Yes, as long as the property allows it and there's an outlet within reach. Please check with your property manager first.

How much does a detail cost?

Pricing depends on your vehicle size and the level of service you choose. Full pricing for Interior, Exterior, and Combo packages is listed on our Services page. If you're not sure which tier fits your car, reach out and we'll help you figure it out.

Will I be charged extra if my vehicle is heavily soiled?

In some cases, yes. Vehicles with excessive dirt, mud, or stains may incur an additional surcharge of $25 to $75 depending on the work required. This is always communicated to you before we begin, never after.

Do you clean the glovebox?

No. We do not open or clean inside the glovebox, out of respect for your personal documents and privacy. The trunk is a different story: every interior package includes a full trunk vacuum, and Deep Interior Restoration covers the full cargo area, including the spare tire well.

Do you remove pet hair?

Yes, as an add-on. Every interior package includes a standard vacuum, which picks up loose hair on the surface. Hair that's woven into carpet and upholstery takes specialized tools and extra time, so full pet hair removal is a separate add-on ($30 to $50 depending on how much there is). Just mention it when you book.

What's the difference between your Interior package tiers?

Basic covers a full vacuum and wipe-down. Standard adds carpet and seat shampoo with extraction, steam cleaning of crevices, and UV protectant. Deep goes further with full steam sanitation, seatbelt cleaning, and detailing every compartment. Full breakdown of what's included in each tier is on our Services page.

What's the difference between a quick car wash and a full detail?

A car wash cleans the surface. A detail goes deeper. We shampoo and extract carpets and seats, steam clean crevices and vents, condition leather, and treat and protect every surface so it lasts. A wash makes your car look clean for a day. A detail actually removes what's built up over months and protects against the next round of wear.
