FAQs.

Is a full detail cheaper than booking interior and exterior separately?

Yes. For a car or sedan, Refresh saves $15, Complete saves $30 and Ultimate saves $40 compared with booking the same interior and exterior services separately. Larger vehicles save the same or more.

Which combo is most popular?

Full Detail Complete: the Standard Interior Detail (shampoo, extraction, steam cleaning, UV protectant) plus the Full Exterior Detail (bug and tar removal, trim protection, spray sealant).

Can I add services to a combo?

Yes. Any add-on, like pet hair removal, leather conditioning, headliner cleaning or paint decontamination, can be added to a combo.

How do I pay?

Payment is due at pickup. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay and Google Pay. No deposit is needed to book.
