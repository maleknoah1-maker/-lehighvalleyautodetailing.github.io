Car Care Guide

5 easy ways to keep your car clean between details.

A detail resets your car. These five habits keep it that way longer, take a few minutes a week, and make your next detail faster and cheaper.

By Noah Malek, owner · October 15, 2026

1. Keep a small trash bag in the car

Most messy interiors start with wrappers, receipts and cups that have nowhere to go. A small bag on the back of a seat or in the door pocket, emptied every time you get gas, keeps the floor clear.

2. Shake out the floor mats every week

Most of the dirt in a car comes in on your shoes. Pulling the front mats and shaking them out takes a minute and stops sand and grit from grinding into the carpet underneath. In winter, rubber all-weather mats are worth it.

3. Wipe the high-touch spots

The steering wheel, shifter, door handles and screen collect oils from your hands. A quick wipe with an interior cleaner and a microfiber cloth every week or two keeps them from getting sticky and shiny. Skip household glass cleaners with ammonia on screens.

4. Wash the outside the safe way

Every two weeks or so, wash with two buckets, a microfiber mitt and a soft towel to dry. Brush car washes leave swirl marks over time. Here's why automatic washes can be rough on paint.

5. Deal with spills and spots right away

Coffee, bird droppings, tree sap and bug splatter are all easier to remove when they're fresh. Bird droppings and bugs can etch into the clear coat if they bake in the sun. Keep a microfiber towel and a small bottle of quick detailer in the trunk.

How often to get a professional detail

With these habits, most daily drivers do well with a full interior and exterior detail two to four times a year, often in spring after winter salt and again in the fall. Families, pet owners and people who commute a lot may want it more often.

Questions

Common questions.

How often should I get my car detailed?

Most daily drivers do well with a full detail two to four times a year, often in spring and fall. Pets, kids and long commutes can mean more often.

What should I keep in my car to keep it clean?

A small trash bag, a microfiber cloth, a small bottle of interior cleaner or quick detailer, and rubber floor mats in winter.

How do I stop my car carpet from getting dirty?

Use floor mats, shake them out weekly, and vacuum before dirt gets ground in. All-weather rubber mats help a lot in winter.

Is it bad to leave bird droppings on my car?

Yes. Bird droppings and bug splatter can etch into the clear coat, especially in the sun. Remove them gently as soon as you can.
