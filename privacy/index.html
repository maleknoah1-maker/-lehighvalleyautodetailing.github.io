Privacy
Policy.

Last updated October 11, 2026

Lehigh Valley Auto Detailing ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information You Give Us

When you submit our booking form, we collect the information you enter: your name, phone number, and optionally your email address, the service you're interested in, your vehicle size, and any notes. If you call, text, or email us, we receive the details you share, such as your number, messages, and any photos of your vehicle.

We use this information only to respond to you, quote and schedule your service, and communicate about your appointment.

Information Collected Automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site, such as which pages are viewed, how visitors arrived, general location (city or region), and device and browser type. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this information. We also measure clicks on our phone and text links. This data helps us improve the site and is not used to identify you personally.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. This site does not set its own cookies. Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser opt-out add-on. The site works normally either way.

Services We Use

We rely on a few trusted providers that process information on our behalf:

  • Formspree delivers booking form submissions to us.
  • Google Analytics provides site usage statistics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps and Google Fonts display the map and fonts on this site, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.
  • Stripe processes card payments. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never stored by us. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with the providers listed above to operate our business, or when required by law.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your contact and service information only as long as we need it to provide our services, stay in touch about your vehicle, and keep normal business records.

Your Choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Email noah@lehighvalleyautodetailing.com or call (484) 719-1144, and we'll take care of it.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
