Winter Salt Protection

Winter salt protection, all season.

Road salt and brine work on your car all winter. We flush them off the underside, out of the jambs and out of the carpet, and time our visits for the days salt does the most damage.

Why It Matters

Why salt removal matters.

  • Salt goes where rust starts. Road salt and brine get thrown under the car and coat the frame, brake lines, suspension, exhaust, wheel wells and rocker panels, where rust starts.
  • Brine leaves a film. A regular wash doesn't fully remove it, so we use a dedicated salt neutralizer.
  • Slush packs into door jambs. We blow them out and dress the rubber seals so doors don't freeze shut.
  • Salt stains carpets and mats. It leaves white rings, and we extract them.
  • Thaw days do the most damage. Salt does the most damage during warmer, wet thaw days, which is exactly when we schedule visits.

Questions

Winter salt questions.

What is a Winter Salt Wash?

A foam pre-wash and hand wash, wheels and tires, hand dry, exterior windows and tire dressing, plus an undercarriage flush with a salt neutralizer, wheel wells and rocker panels, door jambs cleaned, a compressed-air blow-out of the jambs, mirrors, handles and locks, and the rubber seals dressed. It's $90, $105 or $120 depending on vehicle size.

How is the Undercarriage Salt Flush different?

It protects the underside only: an undercarriage rinse, salt neutralizer on the undercarriage, wheel wells, rockers and behind the bumpers, and a full rinse. It is not a body wash. You can add it to any package, including interior-only, for $30, $35 or $40.

Is salt stain extraction included?

Yes, free with Standard Interior, Deep Interior, Full Detail Complete and Full Detail Ultimate. On Basic Interior, Full Detail Refresh and the exterior packages it's $30, $30 or $35 for the floor carpet and mats.

How do the salt plans work?

Each visit is a Winter Salt Wash plus floor mats cleaned. We pick each visit day based on the weather, warm enough to wash and dry safely, and text you 1 to 2 days ahead. Plans are paid at the first visit of each month and are month to month, so you can cancel anytime.

What happens when it's too cold?

We schedule visits on days warm enough to wash and dry safely. Visits missed because of cold weather roll over. There are no refunds for missed visits.

What do I need to start a plan?

Your vehicle needs a Full Exterior Detail, Full Detail Complete or Ultimate, or a Winter Salt Wash at the regular price within the last 30 days.

Can you come to me?

Yes. A single Winter Salt Wash can be booked as mobile with the normal travel fee ($40 in Zone 1, $60 in Zone 2). Mobile plan visits are +$20 per visit in Zone 1 and +$30 in Zone 2, waived when 2 or more plan members are at the same address or on the same street the same day.

What is the Salt Season Pass?

8 bi-weekly visits from December through March, paid up front at the first visit: $520, $600 or $680. It includes 15% off a Full Detail Complete in March or April.
