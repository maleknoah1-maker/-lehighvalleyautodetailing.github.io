Mobile Service

We can come to you.

Every package above is also available as mobile service at your driveway. The package price stays the same, plus a travel fee for your zone.

Travel fees

ZoneAreaTravel fee
Zone 1Coopersburg, Center Valley, Hellertown, Quakertown, Fountain Hill, Emmaus, Bethlehem, Allentown, Wescosville, Pennsburg, Macungie, Perkasie, Catasauqua, Whitehall and Trexlertown (within about 15 miles of Coopersburg)+$40
Zone 215 to 25 miles from Coopersburg, including Breinigsville, Coplay, Nazareth, Northampton and Easton+$60
Beyond 25 milesMobile is not available. Drop-off in Coopersburg is still available.N/A
  • Mobile price = the package price + the travel fee for your zone. Package and add-on prices are the same as drop-off.
  • Second vehicle at the same address: no extra travel fee.
  • Maintenance Wash & Dry is not available as a standalone mobile booking. Pair it with any interior package (that's the Full Detail Refresh).
  • Mobile service is available within 25 miles of Coopersburg. Outside that area, drop-off is still available.

What we need from you

  • A driveway or parking spot where you're allowed to have work done. Some apartment complexes and HOAs don't allow it, so please check first.
  • An outdoor electrical outlet within 50 feet of the vehicle.
  • A working outdoor water spigot (exterior services only).
  • Exterior mobile services depend on the weather. If it's raining or below 40°F, we'll reschedule at no charge, or you can switch to drop-off.

Questions

Pricing questions.

How much does car detailing cost?

Interior packages start at $80, exterior packages at $50, and full details (inside and out) at $115. Every price depends on vehicle size: Car / Sedan, SUV / Truck, or Large SUV / Van.

Is a full detail cheaper than booking interior and exterior separately?

Yes. Each Full Detail combo costs less than its interior and exterior packages booked separately. You save $15 to $25 on the Refresh, $30 to $40 on the Complete, and $40 to $55 on the Ultimate, depending on vehicle size.

Do you offer mobile detailing?

Yes. Every package is available at your driveway for the same price plus a travel fee: $40 in most of the Lehigh Valley, or $60 for 15 to 25 miles from Coopersburg. See mobile pricing.

Do I have to pay a deposit?

No. There is no deposit and no cancellation fee. Payment is due at pickup for drop-off, or when the service is complete for mobile.

Is there an extra charge for a very dirty car?

Heavily soiled vehicles may have an added charge of $25 to $75. We always tell you before we begin, never after. Pet hair is a separate add-on ($30 to $50), not a surcharge.

Which package should I choose?

Text a photo of your vehicle to (484) 719-1144 and we'll recommend the right one. Our most popular package is the Full Detail Complete.
