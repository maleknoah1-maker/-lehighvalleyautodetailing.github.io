Before You Book

Service policies.

Last updated October 13, 2026

Straightforward terms so you know exactly what to expect when you book with us. If anything here is unclear, call or text (484) 719-1144.

Booking & Appointments

All services are by appointment only. You can request a time through our booking form, by phone, or by text, and we'll confirm a time with you directly.

No deposit is required to book.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

There is no cancellation fee and no required notice period. If your plans change, we appreciate a quick call or text so we can offer the time to another customer.

Drop-Off & Pickup

We are based in Coopersburg, PA. You bring your vehicle to us, and the exact drop-off address is provided when your appointment is confirmed.

There is no pickup deadline and no storage fee. We'll let you know when your vehicle is ready.

Mobile Service

We also offer mobile detailing at your driveway. Mobile pricing is the package price plus a travel fee for your zone.

ZoneAreaTravel fee
Zone 1Coopersburg, Center Valley, Quakertown, Emmaus, Allentown and Bethlehem (about 15 miles from Coopersburg)+$40
Zone 215 to 25 miles from Coopersburg, for example Easton, Nazareth and Northampton+$60
Beyond 25 milesMobile is not available. Drop-off in Coopersburg is still available.N/A

A second vehicle at the same address has no extra travel fee. Maintenance Wash & Dry is not available as a standalone mobile booking. Pair it with any interior package (the Full Detail Refresh). Mobile service is available within 25 miles of Coopersburg. Outside that area, drop-off is still available.

For a mobile appointment, please have ready:

  • A driveway or parking spot where you're allowed to have work done. Some apartment complexes and HOAs don't allow it, so please check first.
  • An outdoor electrical outlet within 50 feet of the vehicle.
  • A working outdoor water spigot (exterior services only).
  • Exterior mobile services depend on the weather. If it's raining or below 40°F, we'll reschedule at no charge, or you can switch to drop-off.

If we arrive and can't access the vehicle, power, or water, we'll work with you to reschedule. See mobile detailing for full details.

Pricing & Payment

Package and add-on prices are listed on our Services page and depend on vehicle size and the service you choose.

Vehicles with excessive dirt, mud, or stains may need extra work, which can add a surcharge of $25 to $75. Any surcharge is discussed with you before we begin, never after. Embedded pet hair is handled by the Pet Hair Removal add-on rather than a surcharge.

Payment is due at pickup for drop-off, or when the service is complete for mobile. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Card payments are processed securely by Stripe.

Your Vehicle & Belongings

Please remove valuables and personal items before your appointment. We do not open or clean inside the glovebox, out of respect for your personal documents and privacy.

We treat every vehicle with care. We are not responsible for pre-existing damage, such as scratches, chips, dents, or worn or failing interior materials. If we notice something like this, we'll point it out to you.

Results

We use professional-grade equipment and products and give every vehicle our full attention. Some stains, odors, and wear are permanent, though, and can't be fully removed. If we expect a problem area won't come out completely, we'll tell you up front.

Satisfaction & Concerns

If something isn't right after you get your vehicle back, let us know. There is no time limit on raising a concern. Call or text (484) 719-1144 or email noah@lehighvalleyautodetailing.com, and we'll work with you to make it right.

Changes to These Policies

We may update these policies from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when they were last changed. The policies in effect when you book apply to that appointment.
